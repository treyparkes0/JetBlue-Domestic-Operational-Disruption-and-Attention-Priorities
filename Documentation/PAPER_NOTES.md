# Paper notes

Draft paragraphs for the paper. These are not part of the paper. Checked 16 November 2026 against `data/raw/jetblue_b6_clean.csv`. Late-arrival rates are late arrivals divided by operated flights. Arrival-delay minutes are the floored field on operated flights.

## Sunday is a winter gap, not a July gap

Sunday’s late-arrival gap is large in winter and almost gone in July and August. With 2020 removed, Sunday’s late-arrival rate sits above the other days by 7.8 points in January, 5.9 in February, 6.7 in March, 5.6 in November, and 4.5 in December. In July the gap is 0.9 points, 35.7% against 34.7% on the other days. In August it is 0.1 point. In September Sunday is 1.0 point lower than the other days. July is a high late-arrival month on every day of the week. A Sunday review belongs with the winter operation. It is not a way to explain July.

## 2022–2025 fifth place

In 2022 through 2025, the same four origins stay at the top by arrival-delay minutes: JFK, Boston, Orlando, and Fort Lauderdale. Inside that window Orlando moves ahead of Fort Lauderdale. LaGuardia replaces San Juan as fifth, with 1,050,166 minutes against San Juan’s 1,008,950. Over the full window San Juan is fifth and LaGuardia is seventh. The swap is a window check. It does not change the first review, which is still JFK and Boston on the full window.
